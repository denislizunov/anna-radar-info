Editorial decisions

The operator reviews signals and uses them as research input. ANNA Radar does not publish or modify YouTube content.

Current scope

What the tool does

  • Maintains operator-curated groups of relevant cooking channels.
  • Collects daily snapshots of permitted public video statistics.
  • Excludes Shorts from the current long-form analysis.
  • Highlights new-video momentum and renewed interest in older videos.
  • Supports human review; it does not make automated publishing decisions.

Compliance status

ANNA Radar currently operates in a conservative standard mode. Public YouTube API data is refreshed or deleted within 30 days. Advanced cross-channel derived analytics and longer retention remain disabled unless and until Google grants the applicable approval.
