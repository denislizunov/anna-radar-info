Privacy Policy

This policy explains how ANNA Radar handles information when using YouTube API Services. ANNA Radar is a private, single-operator internal tool. This public website is informational and does not provide user accounts.

1. Information the tool processes

ANNA Radar may retrieve permitted public YouTube channel and long-form video information, including channel and video identifiers, titles, publication dates, thumbnails, durations, and public view counts. The operator manually selects or approves the channels included in monitoring.

If owner-authorized YouTube Analytics access is introduced for channels managed by the operator, ANNA Radar may also process the metrics expressly authorized through Google OAuth. Such access is limited to the operator’s own approved channels.

2. How information is used

The information is used only for internal market research, performance comparison, seasonality review, and editorial decision support. ANNA Radar does not sell data, build advertising profiles, publish collected datasets, or allow third parties to access the local dashboard.

3. Local storage and security

The application database, API credentials, and collected data are stored on the operator’s local computer. This informational website does not contain the application, credentials, collected YouTube data, analytics dashboards, cookies, advertising pixels, or visitor analytics.

4. Retention and refresh

In standard operating mode, stored public YouTube API data is refreshed or deleted no later than 30 calendar days after retrieval, in accordance with applicable YouTube API Services policies. Records are subject to per-record expiry and are not extended through backup rotation. Advanced derived analytics and longer retention are disabled unless the required Google approval applies.

5. Sharing and disclosure

ANNA Radar does not sell, rent, or share YouTube API data with data brokers, advertisers, or the public. Information may be disclosed only when required by law or to protect the security and integrity of the tool, subject to applicable policy and legal requirements.

6. Google and YouTube

ANNA Radar uses YouTube API Services. Use of those services is also governed by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where OAuth access is used, the operator can review or revoke ANNA Radar’s access through Google’s security permissions page.

7. Deletion and contact requests

There are no public user accounts in ANNA Radar. Questions, deletion requests, or privacy concerns can be submitted through the public repository’s issue tracker. Do not include API keys, account credentials, or other sensitive personal information in a public issue.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the tool’s scope or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current version.
